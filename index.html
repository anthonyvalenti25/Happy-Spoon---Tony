How it started

Four friends. One nightly dessert problem.

1234

“We loved dessert, cared about our nutrition, and were tired of choosing between the two.”

So four fraternity brothers started building the yogurt they wanted in their own refrigerators: a high-protein option with the unapologetic flavors of the dessert aisle. Happy Spoon is for everyone whose sweet tooth and goals refuse to break up.
