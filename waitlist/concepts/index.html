Five ways to take the stage

Each concept is a single phone screen with no scrolling. The flavors take turns in the featured spot, and the whole screen turns that flavor's ribbon color. Every preview below is live. Open one on your phone to feel it properly: swipe the tubs, tap a tub, or tap the bars at the top to jump.

Lineup: pick a shelf

Lineup is the favorite. These are four ways to style the shelf the tubs wait on. Everything else is the same.

Orbit

A turntable. All five ride a ring that turns toward you. The tub at the front is full size and the rest shrink into the back of the ring.

Horizon

A turning planet. The tubs stand on a giant curved surface. Each one rolls up over the horizon to the top, with a soft glow rising behind it like a sunrise.

03

Runway

A sideways glide. The tubs slide across in a line. The one that reaches the middle steps up onto a plinth, its name passes behind it, and its color sweeps across the screen.

04

Drop

A landing. The old tub falls away and the next one drops in from above. When it lands, its color floods out from that spot to fill the screen.

Lineup

Taking turns, literally. All five wait on a shelf. One at a time, a tub leaps up to center stage in front of a glowing disc, then hops back into its place in line.
