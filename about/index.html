Hi, we're Chase, Tony, Joey and Carter, the four co-founders of Happy Spoon.

We're four college roommates who care about staying in shape and pay close attention to what we eat. We also genuinely love dessert.

That made dessert a frustrating part of the day. We wanted to enjoy the treats life has to offer without feeling like we were compromising the work we'd put in.

One night, while we were sitting together brainstorming healthier snack ideas, someone mentioned dessert yogurt. The idea immediately got all four of us interested.

From then on, we used every spare moment—between classes, workouts and time at home—to bounce ideas off one another and figure out how to make it real.

Those conversations became Happy Spoon: high-protein dessert yogurt made in the flavors we actually crave, including Chocolate Fudge, Cookies & Cream, Salted Caramel, Mint Chip and Cookie Dough.

What started as an idea between roommates has become a shared mission to make dessert something you can look forward to and feel good about. We're excited to share it with you.
