Hi, we're Chase, Tony, Joey and Carter, the four co-founders of Happy Spoon.

We're seniors in college, and we all live in the same house. Four roommates, one kitchen, and a fridge that's never quite big enough.

All four of us love dessert. All four of us also pay attention to what we eat. So we kept running into the same problem: the late-night craving would hit, and the options were a dessert that undid the whole day, or a "healthy" snack that tasted like a compromise.

One night we were sitting around the living room throwing out business ideas, the way roommates do. Most of them didn't survive the first laugh. Then someone asked: what if dessert was high-protein? Not a protein bar pretending to be a cookie, but something you'd actually look forward to. Thick, creamy and spoonable. Yogurt that eats like dessert.

The room went quiet for a second, and then all four of us said the same thing: "I would eat that."

That was the start of Happy Spoon: high-protein dessert yogurt in the flavors we actually crave. Chocolate Fudge, Cookies & Cream, Salted Caramel, Mint Chip and Cookie Dough, in big 32 oz tubs.

We're building it the same way we started it: from our house, between classes, as four friends who wanted something better to eat and decided to make it ourselves.

That's what Happy Spoon is all about. We hope you love it as much as we do.

Chase, Tony, Joey & CarterCo-founders, Happy Spoon
