01 · Idea

Warm colors, a friendly voice, one smile.

The cream, cocoa and coral straight off the tub (01), talking like a friend who's into fitness but still orders dessert (08). The smile from under the logo becomes our one graphic shape, always in coral.

Warm by default.

Cream backgrounds, cocoa words, coral for the one thing to tap. Flavor colors stay with the product.

One smile per layout.

Besides the logo, every page, post or email gets exactly one smile: an underline, a crop or a button.

Talk like a friend.

Warm, upbeat and honest. Encouraging, never preachy, never punny.

09 · Rules

The short version.

    Always

  • Cream backgrounds, cocoa words.
  • One coral thing to tap per screen.
  • One smile per layout, besides the logo.
  • Sound like a friend: warm, honest, encouraging.
  • Sign emails and cards as the four of us.

    Never

  • Flavor colors as backgrounds or decoration.
  • A smile in any color but coral (or cream on coral).
  • Mascots, faces on tubs, stickers or doodles.
  • Puns, guilt language or diet lectures.
  • Print a nutrition number we haven't confirmed.
