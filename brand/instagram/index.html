Flavor Burst

Our Instagram style. A real dessert breaks open on its flavor's color, swirls into yogurt, and the tub comes out of the swirl. Every reel ends on the same card.

01 · The look

Five things make it ours.

This is Flavor Flood in motion. If a post breaks one of these, it doesn't go up.

One flavor, one color, full bleed.

The flavor's flood fills the whole frame: a seamless studio color, no gradient, no props, no set. Never two flavors in one post, except the lineup.

The real dessert is the hook.

The first second is the dessert the flavor is named after: fudge, a cookie, caramel, mint and chocolate, cookie dough. Macro, glossy, sharp. It stops the scroll before anyone knows it's yogurt.

Break, swirl, reveal.

Slow-motion break, then the pieces fall into a yogurt swirl, then the tub rises out of the swirl. Same three moves every time, so people learn the pattern.

Same ending, every time.

White logo, tub, HIGH PROTEIN DESSERT, the numbers, one button, and the lineup strip along the bottom. The ending is what makes five different reels look like one brand.

Numbers go big.

Protein and calories are the headline, not fine print. Until the recipes are final, use the no-numbers ending (see Before posting).

02 · The formula

Ten seconds, five beats.

All four reels follow this exact timing. Pick a flavor to see its version.

03 · The end card

Locked. Don't let the AI write it.

The video tool makes great dessert shots but invents its own text, and the endings came out different every time. From now on, generate the video with no text and put our end card on top in CapCut.

What drifted in the first four
  • Chocolate Fudge says HIGH PROTEIN LOW CALORIE and has no numbers or button.
  • Cookie Dough puts the text above the tub, adds periods ("Dessert."), uses a brown button, and the last second drops the logo.
  • Mint Chip and Cookies & Cream use different text sizes and button sizes.
  • The lineup strip is in a different order in each one (it should always run Fudge → Dough).
  • Every reel used the right flood color, a white logo and the real tub. Keep all of that.
The spec
  • Size1080 × 1920, 9:16
  • LogoWhite, top center
  • HeadlineHIGH PROTEIN DESSERT, Archivo Expanded Black
  • NumbersProtein first, then calories
  • ButtonJoin the waitlist, in the flavor's dark button color
  • StripAll five, Fudge → Dough
  • On screenLast 3 seconds, fades in over 0.3 s

The overlays have a transparent middle so the tub from the video shows through. Files are in assets/instagram/end-cards/. The full cards double as Stories. Need a different button? Open end-card.html with ?cta=box and screenshot it.

06 · Captions
Name it. Back it up. Send them.

    
Flavor name, then the numbers (once they're real), then one short line with a little fun, then the button. Two to four lines, three hashtags max.

  

    

    
Rules
      
    
        
  • Start with the flavor name. It's the first thing people read.
    • 
        
  • Jokes are about the food or the craving, never about anyone's body or diet.
    • 
        
  • End with the action: "Join the waitlist, link in bio."
    • 
        
  • Hashtags: #HappySpoon #HighProteinDessert, plus one for the flavor.
    • 
        
  • No flavor descriptions or taglines under the name.
    • 
        
  • Don't say cup, thick, creamy or sinful.
    • 
        
  • Don't name other companies' cookies or candy (say "cookies & cream", not the brand name).

08 · Before posting
Three checks before going public.

  

    
1. Real numbers only
20 g protein and 180 calories are placeholders. Once a post is public, people screenshot it, so wait until the recipe's nutrition panel is final. Until then, use the no-numbers overlay.

    
2. Check "low calorie"
"High protein" and "low calorie" are FDA label terms with set minimums, and ads are held to the same claims. "Low calorie" usually means 40 calories or less per serving, so a 180-calorie serving likely can't use it. That's why the locked line is HIGH PROTEIN DESSERT. The Chocolate Fudge reel and the five static posts say LOW CALORIE, so swap those lines or confirm the term before they go up.

    
3. Label it as AI
These reels are AI-generated and look real, so turn on Instagram's AI label when posting. Once real tubs exist, film real spoonfuls too. Real footage next to the Flavor Burst reels builds trust.
